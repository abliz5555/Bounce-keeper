How to play

  1. 1Hold your phone upright. Tilt it left and right to steer the ball.
  2. 2The paddle moves by itself. Land the ball on it to score 1 point.
  3. 3The game slowly gets harder: the paddle speeds up, shrinks, and wind starts at 20 points.
  4. 4Rewards float down from the top. Steer the ball into the good ones and avoid the spiky ones.
  5. 5After 20 points a treasure chest may fall once per game. Catch it and open it after the game for coins, or rarely diamonds.
  6. 6If the ball falls, you can revive with an ad or with diamonds.
  7. 7Spend coins on skins, upgrades and boosts. Legendary skins need diamonds.

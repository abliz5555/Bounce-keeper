How to play

🎯 The goal

The paddle moves by itself. Steer the ball so it lands on the paddle. Each bounce is +1 point. If the ball falls past the paddle, the game ends. Beat your best score!

🎮 Controls

  • Swipe (easiest): slide your finger in the glowing strip under the paddle. Hold to one side to keep pushing.
  • Tilt: tilt your phone left and right.
  • Change it any time in Settings.

📈 Getting harder

  • The paddle slowly speeds up and gets smaller.
  • From 20 points, wind pushes the ball sideways.
  • After 100 points the paddle sometimes turns by surprise.

🫧 Falling rewards

Steer the ball into good bubbles. Avoid the spiky ones. Bubbles fade away by the middle of the screen.

🧰 Treasure chests

  • After 20 points a chest may fall once per game. Catch it with the ball.
  • About 8% are Legendary chests. You can also buy one in the Shop for 100 diamonds.
  • The chest opens right after the game. Its glow hints at the prize: gold coins, blue diamonds, green Special skin, purple Legendary skin.
  • Normal chest: coins, sometimes diamonds or a Special skin.
  • Legendary chest: Legendary or Special skins, many diamonds or lots of coins.
  • A skin you already own gives back 80% of its price.

✨ Skins

  • Change your Ball, Paddle and World with the tiles on the start page.
  • Normal skins: coins or one ad. Special: coins. Legendary: diamonds.
  • Special and Legendary skins can be upgraded to Level 3 for bigger effects.
  • Free skin sets: score 50, 75, 100 and 125 in one game.
  • Mythic Celestia set: make 150, 250 and 400 bounces in one game.

⚡ Boosts

Permanent upgrades: bigger paddle, longer power-ups, coin magnet, fewer bad bubbles, Big ball size and a Shield that comes back.

🎁 Rewards

  • Daily gift: a 7-day streak. Day 7 is the best.
  • Monthly challenge: play on 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 days in a month.
  • Milestones: reach scores from 20 to 1000 for coins, chests, skins and badges.

❤️ Revive

When the ball falls, revive with a free ad (once per game) or diamonds (15, 30, 60). Up to 3 revives.

🏆 Ranks

Your 10 best games, badges and stats. World ranking comes with Apple Game Center in the App Store version.
